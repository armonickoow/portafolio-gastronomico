Acerca de Mí

I am a passionate and results-driven Head Chef & Kitchen Manager with over 8 years of hands-on experience leading high-volume culinary operations, designing creative menus, and optimizing kitchen efficiency. Currently based in Melbourne, VIC under a Working Holiday Visa, I hold full work rights and full-time availability.
